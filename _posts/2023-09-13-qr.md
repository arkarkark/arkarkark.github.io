I've been trying to spread the word about [wifi QR codes](https://www.qrstuff.com/type/wifi), everyone should put one up for their guest network. It took me a long time to look it up but [here is the format](https://pocketables.com/2022/01/how-to-format-that-wifi-qr-code-in-plain-text.html) of it. and there's also a [format for contacts](https://pocketables.com/2022/08/how-to-format-vcard-qr-code-in-plain-text.html) too. which means you can probably use the [google chart api](https://developers.google.com/chart/infographics/docs/qr_codes) to make them quite easily. I like QR codes. (edited)

## Wifi Format

```
WIFI:S:<SSID>;T:<WEP|WPA|blank>;P:<PASSWORD>;H:<true|false|blank>;;
```

WIFI:S:my-guest;T:WPA;P:let there be cake;;

## vcard format

```
BEGIN:VCARD
VERSION:4.0
EMAIL;type=WORK:John@example.com
EMAIL;type=HOME:work@example.com
TEL;type=WORK:+1 ‪(408) 555-1234
NOTE:here's some random text to throw into the contact information
ADR;type=WORK:;;123 fake street;Boston;MA;02555;USA
CATEGORIES: blogger, internet troll
TZ:America/Boston
URL:https://www.example.com
FN:John Q Public
N:John Public
END:VCARD
```
